This Privacy Policy explains how MPMF Game (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you download, install, or play PAW ARMY: ISLAND CONQUEST (“the Game”). Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before optional analytics or advertising-related processing.

This policy primarily covers the mobile releases of the Game. The information handled depends on the version, the services enabled, and your privacy choices. If a web edition is offered through a third-party gaming platform, that platform may also process information under its own privacy policy; platform-specific services apply only where enabled.

01 Information we handle

Gameplay and app activity

Depending on the version of the Game and services enabled, information may include installation and launch events, sessions, battle or level starts and results, mission progress, unit selections and upgrades, unlocks, virtual coins, reward-related events, and technical events used to understand errors and event delivery. Gameplay events, where collected, relate to the Game’s island battles, army progression, and rewards.

Device and technical information

We and enabled service providers may process app version, device model, operating system, language or region, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, network information, performance and diagnostic data, and device or advertising identifiers where permitted.

Information stored on your device

Where local saving is supported, the Game may store progress, level unlocks, unit upgrades, virtual coins, rewards, and settings on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game may remove local saves unless your device or platform provides a backup.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to share.

02 How we use information

We use information as needed to operate the Game, retain and display supported progress, provide gameplay rewards, respond to support and privacy requests, understand how battles and progression features are used, improve performance and game balance, investigate errors or abuse, and—where enabled—measure app installations and the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for a materially different use.

03 Adjust analytics and attribution

The Game uses Adjust for analytics and attribution in mobile releases where the integration is configured and enabled. Adjust may process installation, launch, session, in-game event, device, IP-address, identifier, attribution, and diagnostic information for analytics, attribution, advertising measurement, and fraud prevention.

Event parameters may describe battle or level identifiers, mission results, progress, unit upgrades, virtual coins, or rewards. The exact events depend on the released version and enabled configuration. The presence of an integration does not mean every possible event or identifier is collected.

Adjust’s handling is described in its Privacy Policy.

04 Planned Meta / Facebook SDK

We plan to integrate the Meta (formerly Facebook) SDK in a future release. The processing described in this section may begin only if the SDK is actually integrated and enabled. This planned integration does not mean the current version sends information to Meta.

When enabled, Meta may receive installation, launch, session, and selected in-game event information, together with app version, device and operating-system details, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, and advertising or other permitted identifiers. These data may be used to measure advertising performance, attribute installs and events, provide aggregated analytics, and help detect fraud.

Access to an advertising identifier or other tracking will follow applicable device permissions and consent requirements. The SDK does not give us access to your Facebook password or private messages. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

05 Sharing and third-party services

Information may be processed by providers that support analytics or attribution, app distribution, diagnostics, security, or customer support, only as relevant to a feature or service in use. Adjust is described above; Meta would become a recipient only after its planned SDK is enabled in a released version. The app store or operating-system provider may handle information under its own privacy policy when you install, update, or use the Game.

Where an online web edition uses a third-party gaming platform, that platform’s enabled services may handle platform account information, game progress, advertising interactions, and technical data under its own privacy policy. This does not mean those platform services are available in every edition of the Game.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users and our rights, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable law.

We do not directly sell personal information for money. Some advertising measurement or attribution activities may be treated as “sharing,” “sale,” or targeted advertising under certain laws; the applicable choices are described below.

06 Consent and device choices

Where consent is required for optional analytics or advertising measurement, we will request it through the Game or applicable platform controls. You may use your device’s privacy settings to limit or reset advertising identifiers, where those controls are available.

Declining optional tracking should not prevent access to the Game’s core play. Some processing required to operate the Game or respond to requests may continue as permitted by law. You may contact us to ask about withdrawing consent or exercising the rights described below.

07 Retention, security, and international processing

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, security, dispute resolution, or legal requirements. Where local saves are supported, that data remains on your device until it is changed, cleared, or removed. Service providers may have separate retention practices.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country; where required, appropriate legal safeguards apply.

08 Your privacy rights and deletion requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also complain to a relevant data-protection authority.

Contact xiongchang@mpmfgame.com with the subject “PAW ARMY: ISLAND CONQUEST Privacy Request.” For deletion, use “PAW ARMY: ISLAND CONQUEST Data Deletion Request.” Include enough information to identify your request, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive data. We may verify your identity and retain information where legally required.

09 Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable child-privacy laws. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided information without legally required authorization, contact us. We will review the request and take appropriate action.

10 Changes and third-party links

The Game may link to third-party services governed by their own policies. We may update this policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will revise the “Last updated” date and give additional notice or seek consent when required.
